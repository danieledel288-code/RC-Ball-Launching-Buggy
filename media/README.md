## wifi_qr.png

Scan with the iPhone Camera app to join the ESP32's `PickleballTrainer` network with no typing. Encodes `WIFI:S:PickleballTrainer;T:WPA;P:launch123;;` (verified by decoding the image back, not just by the encode succeeding). Regenerate if the AP SSID or password in `flywheel_control.ino` / `train.ino` ever changes - the two aren't linked automatically.
